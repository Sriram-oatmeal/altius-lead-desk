BRIGADE ALTIUS – AUTOMATIC LEAD CAPTURE SETUP (about 15 minutes)
=================================================================

How it works
------------
Buyer fills the form on your landing page
  -> the lead is saved in your Google Sheet straight away
  -> you get an email alert with the buyer's number and a WhatsApp link
  -> the lead appears in your Lead Desk within 30 seconds, status "New",
     next action "Call within 5 minutes", marked NEW
Every change you make in the Lead Desk is saved back to the same sheet,
so your phone and laptop always show the same list.

STEP 1 – Create the Google Sheet
--------------------------------
1. On a computer, open  sheets.new  (signed in to your Google account).
2. Name it "Altius Leads".

STEP 2 – Add the script
-----------------------
1. In the sheet, click Extensions -> Apps Script.
2. Delete everything in the editor.
3. Open 3-google-sheet-script/Code.gs from this folder (Notepad is fine),
   copy all of it and paste it into the editor. Click the save icon.

STEP 3 – Run setup once
-----------------------
1. In the function menu at the top, choose "setup", then click Run.
2. Google asks for permission: Review permissions -> choose your account
   -> "Advanced" -> "Go to Altius Leads (unsafe)" -> Allow.
   (It says unsafe only because you wrote the script yourself. It needs the
   sheet, and email so it can send you lead alerts.)
3. Back in the sheet you will see a "Leads" tab with column headings.

STEP 4 – Publish it as a web app
--------------------------------
1. Click Deploy -> New deployment.
2. Click the gear icon next to "Select type" -> Web app.
3. Execute as: Me.   Who has access: Anyone.
4. Click Deploy, then copy the Web app URL. It ends in /exec.
5. Test: paste the URL into a browser. You should see {"ok":true,...}

STEP 5 – Connect the landing page
---------------------------------
1. Open 1-landing-page/index.html in Notepad.
2. Find the line:   leadsEndpoint: "PASTE_WEB_APP_URL_HERE"
3. Replace PASTE_WEB_APP_URL_HERE with your /exec URL (keep the quotes). Save.
   (Or send the URL to Claude and it will do this for you.)
4. Drag the 1-landing-page folder onto app.netlify.com/drop
   (or onto your existing site's Deploys page to update it).

STEP 6 – Connect the Lead Desk
------------------------------
1. Drag the 2-lead-desk folder onto app.netlify.com/drop as a separate site.
2. Open it, paste the /exec URL and the access key above, click Connect.
3. Do this once on each phone or computer you use.

STEP 7 – Test it
----------------
Fill the form on your landing page with your own name and number.
Within a minute you should see it in the sheet, get the email,
and see it in the Lead Desk marked NEW.


Tracking which ads bring leads
------------------------------
Add these to the end of your landing page link in each ad:
  Meta ads:    ?utm_source=fb&utm_campaign=altius_nov
  Google ads:  ?utm_source=google&utm_campaign=altius_search
  WhatsApp:    ?utm_source=whatsapp
The Lead Desk then fills the Source column automatically and shows the campaign.

If you change Code.gs later
---------------------------
Deploy -> Manage deployments -> pencil icon -> Version: New version -> Deploy.
This keeps the same URL.
